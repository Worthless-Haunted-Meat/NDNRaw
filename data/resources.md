# Resources

External programs, organizations, and services referred by the community.
These are adjacent to the idea, not substitutes for it — the group is organic
and peer-driven; these serve the employment and support arc around it.

## Idea fragments worth keeping

- **Strength-based employment** (Commenter D): jobs work when built on what the
  person loves; neurodivergent applicants are increasingly a sought-after category.
- **Low-interaction careers** (Commenter A): teaching, accounting,
  contract/patent law, delivery, software/web development.
- **Hobby commerce on-ramp** (Commenter E): buying and selling within a
  hyperfocus hobby (e.g., Facebook Marketplace) as low-pressure practice in
  transactions and negotiation.
