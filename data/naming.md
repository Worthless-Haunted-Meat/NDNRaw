# Naming

Status: **leaning hard toward The 24K Network** — validate with the group before locking.

Rules:
- No "autism," "spectrum," "special," or "abilities" in the name.
- Gender-unbiased: parents and young adults, not dad/son-coded.
- Prefer names that don't depend on a tech unit aging poorly.

## Leading pick: The 24K Network (or 24K)

**Front door meaning: 24 karat — the pure stuff.** The higher-resolution /
bandwidth story from the founding post is origin lore, not the everyday
explanation. That keeps the name from going stale when modem numbers do.

| Strength | Why |
|----------|-----|
| Double meaning | Karat (durable) + founding bandwidth handshake (optional) |
| Short | Says well: "twenty-four K" / "24K Dallas" |
| Sub-brands | Gold Record (catalog), first gold (parent-seeded gigs) |

**Rejected for the door, kept for the story:** 2048K — funny escalation of the
bandwidth joke, loses the karat read, harder to say, still tech-dated.

## Other candidates (backup if the group rejects 24K)
