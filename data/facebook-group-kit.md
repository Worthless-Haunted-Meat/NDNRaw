## Group description (About)

Facebook’s description field is short. Use this:

```
For parents and neurodivergent young adults who know the usual networking
rooms aren’t built for how we run.

We meet in two rooms: young adults together (games, hobbies, whatever they
love — networking as a byproduct), parents in the other (support, strategy,
and staying curious about their superpowers).

Organic. Not a program. Not therapy. Moms, dads, daughters, sons, grandparents.

If that hits — you’re in the right place.
```

**Optional one-liner** (if Facebook asks for a short “group purpose”):
```
Two-room meetups so neurodivergent young adults can network at their bandwidth — and parents don’t have to do it alone.
```

**Suggested settings**
- Privacy: **Private**
- Visibility: **Visible** (searchable) until you have enough people, then hide if you want
- Who can post: Members (or Admin + members after approval — your call)

## Group rules (add in Facebook Rules)

Keep them short. Facebook shows these on join.

**1. Parents and young adults — all genders**
Moms, dads, daughters, sons, grandparents. This isn’t a dad-only or son-only thing.

**2. Two lanes**
Young-adult space stays light: hang out, interests, games. Heavy diagnosis debates and “fix them” talk stay with the parents.

**3. No lazy / apathetic labels**
We don’t shame our kids into motivation. We build a room that fits how they work.

**4. What’s said here stays here**
People are trusting you with their family. Don’t screenshot or share outside without asking.

**5. Organic, not a program**
No curriculum. No pressure. Show up when you can. Be decent.

## Pinned welcome post

Post this, then **Pin to top**.

```
Welcome to NDNGenesis.

Short version of why this exists:

Career stuff runs on networking. Bars, trade shows, crowds — those rooms
hit a lot of our kids like a denial-of-service attack. Not lazy. Not
apathetic. Different bandwidth. We can’t fully speak their language — but
other young adults their age can.

So we do this:

🏠 Room 1 — the young adults
Hang out. Play games. Do what they love. Networking happens as a side effect.

🏠 Room 2 — the parents
We talk. Resources. Strategy. How not to panic. How to notice the
superpowers and maybe, eventually, help them gel into something real
(a band, a crew, a first paid gig).

This group is the online home. The real thing is in-person when we can
make it happen.

A few asks:
1. Introduce yourself below — parent / young adult, city or area, and one
   thing your person loves (or you love).
2. We’re also testing a brand name: 24K (as in 24-karat — the pure stuff).
   Yes / meh / prefer something else — say it in the comments or wait for
   the naming post.
3. If you’re local and want meetup #1, say so. We’re going to do this anyway.

Who’s in?
```
