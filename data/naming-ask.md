# Naming ask — draft for NDNGenesis

Copy, tweak voice, post in the **NDNGenesis** Facebook group.
Goal: gut-check 24K, not invent fifty new names.

---

Working name for this thing: **24K**.

As in 24-karat — the pure stuff. The kids aren't broken; the usual networking
rooms just aren't built for how they run. We're building the other room:
parents in one, young adults in the other, talents that gel, and maybe
eventually real gigs when the right people find each other.

Does **24K** land for you?

React / reply with:
1. **Yes** — keep it
2. **Meh** — doesn't click (say why in one line if you want)
3. **Prefer something else** — drop one alternative

Not looking for a committee. Looking for whether this feels like *us*.

---

## How to use the answers

- Strong yes → lock 24K, move on to meetup #1
- Split / meh → try Wavelength or Side Channel in a second round of two
- Flood of wild new names → thank them, don't chase; decide from the shortlist
