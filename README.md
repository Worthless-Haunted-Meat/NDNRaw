# NDNGenesis

This is the beginning. Not a program. Not a curriculum. Not a guide.

Working name: **NDNGenesis**. Brand lean (not locked): **24K**.

A Facebook group and, when people show up, two rooms in a house — young adults
in one, parents in the other. What happens after that is a function of the
people in it.

## The Core Idea

Career success runs on networking. The rooms where networking usually happens
(bars, trade shows, crowds) hit a lot of neurodivergent young adults like a
denial-of-service attack. Not lazy. Not apathetic. Higher resolution. Different
bandwidth.

> We run at 2400 baud. They run at 24K. We do not stand a chance in providing them
> experiences to deal with that kind of bandwidth. But they have resources that can:
> other young men and women their age.

**The paradox:** the thing that makes a person successful — a network — is the
thing neurodivergence often blocks.

**The move:** peers at their bandwidth, in a room. Parents build the container
and get out of the way. No syllabus. No facilitation script. Genesis.

### Two rooms

| Room | Who | What |
|------|-----|------|
| 1 | Young adults | Whatever they do. Games. Hobbies. Nothing. Networking as a byproduct. |
| 2 | Parents | Talk. Resources. Curiosity about the superpowers. Maybe later, gigs. |

### What this is / isn't

1. **Genesis, not guidance.** We don't tell people how to be here. We open the
   room. The people write the rest.
2. **Organic.** A function of whoever shows up — not an institution.
3. **Interest-first.** Livelihood, if it comes, comes from what they love.
4. **Gender-unbiased.** Moms, dads, daughters, sons, grandparents.
5. **Threshold is real, mentors for edge cases.** Not a program boundary —
   honesty about who the room is for, with lived-experience mentors when needed.

## Why this repo

Capture checkpoints so the idea's evolution is visible. Raw source lives in
`data/raw/`. Ideas that might grow later live in `VISION.md` and
`DELIVERABLES.md` — as possibilities, not a to-do list to impose on anyone.
