# NDNGenesis

A grassroots, dual-sided community for neurodivergent young adults and their parents.
Working group name: **NDNGenesis**. Proposed brand (not locked): **24K**.

This repo is the **checkpoint data-gathering record** for the idea: every signal of
interest, every meetup, every resource, and every lesson gets captured here so the
idea's evolution is documented and measurable.

## The Core Idea

Career success runs on networking — but conventional networking venues (bars, trade
shows, conventions, crowds) are exactly the environments that overwhelm young adults
on the spectrum. They aren't lazy or unmotivated; they perceive the world at a far
higher resolution of detail. Ordinary social environments hit them like a
denial-of-service attack.

> We run at 2400 baud. They run at 24K. We do not stand a chance in providing them
> experiences to deal with that kind of bandwidth. But they have resources that can:
> other young men and women their age.

**The central paradox** this attacks: the very thing that makes a person
successful — building a network — is the very thing neurodivergence prevents.

**The mechanism:** the only people equipped to provide social experiences at that
bandwidth are peers of the same neurotype and age. So we build the container and get
out of the room.

### The two-room structure

A recurring (weekly) gathering:

| Room | Who | What happens |
|------|-----|--------------|
| Room 1 | The young adults | They hang out, play games, do what they love. Networking happens as a *byproduct* — a real peer network forming organically in a sensory-safe substitute for the venues they can't use. |
| Room 2 | The parents | Support and strategy. Sharing resources, comparing notes, staying curious about each kid's superpower — watching it get expressed, forged, maybe even weaponized into a livelihood. |

### Design principles

1. **Organic, not institutional.** The group is "a function of the people in it."
   Not therapy, not a class, not a vocational program.
2. **Interest-first.** Networking and eventually livelihood grow out of what each
   person *loves* doing — not out of skills training.
3. **Peers, not professionals, carry the social load.** Parents build the
   container; the young adults fill it.
4. **Defined threshold, with mentors for special cases.** The core group needs a
   defined threshold of neurodivergence. Special cases (e.g., wheelchair-bound
   autistic adults) are served by bringing in — in person or by Zoom — mentors who
   share that lived experience.
5. **The long arc:** friendship → shared talents → livelihood, without ever making
   it feel like a program.
6. **Gender-unbiased by design.** Moms, dads, daughters, sons, grandparents. The
   idea began in a dads' group, but the model is parents and young adults — full
   stop. Everything we author uses that language.

## Why This Repo Exists

The idea was validated the moment it was posted: parents replied "I'm in" and "your
son should meet my son" unprompted. That's data. This repo gathers it at
**checkpoints** — dated snapshots of where the idea stands — so we can see momentum,
spot patterns, and make decisions from evidence rather than memory.

## Repo Structure

```
README.md                  ← you are here
VISION.md                  ← where this points: scaling model, strength catalog, binding talent
DELIVERABLES.md            ← the concrete artifacts to build, phased by what unblocks meetup #1
data/
  raw/RawIdeas.txt         ← the founding artifact: original post + comments, verbatim
  roster.md                ← who's in: interested families, allies, professionals
  resources.md             ← external programs, orgs, and services worth knowing
  naming.md                ← leading brand pick: 24K; group is NDNGenesis
  naming-ask.md            ← draft post to ask NDNGenesis about the brand
  facebook-group-kit.md    ← cover text, About, rules, pinned welcome (paste-ready)
  channels.md              ← where we live online/offline (NDNGenesis group, etc.)
checkpoints/
  TEMPLATE.md              ← copy this to start a new checkpoint
  2026-08-01-checkpoint-000.md  ← founding
  2026-08-01-checkpoint-001.md  ← vision, naming, deliverables
  2026-08-01-checkpoint-002.md  ← NDNGenesis group live
```

## How to Add a Checkpoint

1. Copy `checkpoints/TEMPLATE.md` to `checkpoints/YYYY-MM-DD-checkpoint-NNN.md`.
2. Fill in what happened since the last checkpoint: new interest, meetups held,
   attendance, what worked, what didn't, decisions made.
3. Update `data/roster.md` and `data/resources.md` if anything changed.
4. Commit. The git history *is* the timeline.
