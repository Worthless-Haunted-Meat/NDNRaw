# Checkpoint 001 — 2026-08-01 (Vision + Naming + Deliverables)

Same day as founding, but the idea moved enough to deserve its own snapshot.

## Since last checkpoint

- **The central paradox was articulated** and became the organizing thesis:
  the very thing that makes a person successful — building a network — is the
  very thing neurodivergence prevents. Everything we build is an attack on
  that paradox.
- **nonPareil studied** ([npusa.org](https://npusa.org/)) and adopted as an
  inspiration, not a template: their proof is that workforce-mimicking
  activities build real experience, and experience raises the odds of success.
  Their difference from us: they are an institution; we are organic,
  parent-built, and free.
- **The scaling model defined** (see `VISION.md`): cells of 5–7 families per
  neighborhood → 10–20 federated cells per city → replicated across cities.
- **The strength catalog defined**: parents observe and record each young
  adult's strengths as they emerge; catalogs roll up across cells so the
  network can see talent no single cell could assemble.
- **The economic engine defined**: parents bind complementary strengths into
  ensembles (five musicians → a band) and act as the first market, paying for
  first gigs. Paid, résumé-worthy experience inside a safe container.
- **Naming explored** (see `data/naming.md`): leading candidate **The 24K
  Network** — from the bandwidth metaphor (2400 baud vs 24K), doubling as
  24 karat gold. No decision yet.
- **Deliverables drafted** (see `DELIVERABLES.md`): the concrete artifacts to
  build, ordered by what unblocks meetup #1 first.

## Decisions made

- The activities in the young adults' room should increasingly mimic real work
  over time (the nonPareil insight), while never feeling like a program.
- Parents are the first market: demand is seeded from inside the network.
- Deliverables are prioritized by what unblocks meetup #1.
- **Gender-unbiased by design.** Not a son-dad thing: moms, dads, daughters,
  sons, grandparents. Authored documents use parent/young-adult language;
  the founding post and roster stay verbatim as historical record. (Added as
  design principle 6 in the README.)
