The idea was posted publicly (a dads' group on Facebook) by Ricky Vega. This
checkpoint captures the state of the idea and the response at the moment of
founding. The verbatim source is preserved in `data/raw/RawIdeas.txt`.

## Since last checkpoint

- Original post written and published. Core concept articulated: the two-room
  weekly gathering, the bandwidth metaphor, peer-to-peer networking as the
  mechanism, parents as container-builders.
- Immediate unprompted validation in the comments: parents volunteering their
  kids, allies offering help, professionals confirming the strategic thesis.
- Scope correction already made by the author: originally framed for dads,
  explicitly widened to moms (and, by the responses, grandparents).
- Same-day evolution (see `VISION.md`): the central paradox articulated
  ("the very thing that makes a person successful — networking — is the very
  thing neurodivergence prevents"), the cell/city scaling model, the strength
  catalog, and parent-seeded first gigs. Inspired in part by studying
  [nonPareil](https://npusa.org/).

## What worked

- **The bandwidth reframe landed.** Removing the moral judgment (lazy, apathetic)
  and explaining *why* parental motivation fails resonated instantly.
- **Both rooms showed demand on their own.** Parents asked for the peer room
  ("your son should meet my son") and the parent room (support/strategy) without
  being sold on either.

## What didn't

- Dads-only framing excluded interested moms and grandparents; corrected same day.

## Decisions made

- Proceed regardless of turnout ("I am going to this anyway").
- The group is organic — a function of the people in it — not a program.
- A threshold of neurodivergence must be defined for the core group; special
  cases handled via mentors with shared lived experience (in person or Zoom).

## Open questions

- When and where is meetup #1? Whose house, or a neutral venue?
- Structured activity (games) vs. fully unstructured for the young adults' room?
- How do interested people who aren't local participate, if at all?
